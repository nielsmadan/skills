# AI Writing Tells — Catalog & Fixes

The pattern reference for `deslop`. Load this while editing. Sources: Wikipedia
"Signs of AI writing", a medRxiv PubMed word-frequency study (15M+ abstracts;
*delves/showcasing/underscores* up to 25× more frequent post-2023), Pangram
Labs' phrase-frequency analysis, and editor/copywriter guides (2024–2026).

## The one principle that matters

**Density, not any single word.** Every word below appears in good human
writing. What marks text as AI is *clusters* of them, used reflexively,
detached from concrete specifics. Edit for clusters, not isolated hits.

**The fix is usually deletion, not synonym-swapping.** These words and frames
exist to *sound* authoritative while saying nothing. A swap table
(delve→explore) handles the obvious cases, but mechanical swapping just
produces *differently* robotic text. The higher-value move: **delete the
inflation and force a concrete specific** — or if no specific exists, cut the
sentence. "Plays a pivotal role in modern healthcare" → either name *what it
does* or delete it.

**Recency weighting.** *delve* and *tapestry* are now so notorious they're
near-certain tells, but also rare enough that good writers already avoid them.
The *live* signals as of 2025–2026 are **showcasing, highlighting, emphasizing,
enhance**. Weight current-era tells over burned ones.

## 1. Single-word tells

### 1a. Flagship words (strongest signals)

| Word | Why it reads as AI | Replace with |
|------|--------------------|--------------|
| delve / delve into | The canonical tell | explore, dig into, look at, examine |
| tapestry / rich tapestry | Empty decorative metaphor | mix, range — or cut |
| testament / a testament to | Inflated significance marker | shows, proves, reflects |
| underscore(s) | Stiff signpost verb | highlight, stress, show |
| showcasing / showcase | Promotional filler verb | shows, presents, features |
| pivotal | Inflated importance | key, central, important |
| realm | Pretentious for "area" | area, field, world |
| landscape (figurative) | Vague abstraction | field, industry, market, scene |
| intricate / intricacies | Padding; implies depth not shown | complex, detailed — or cut |
| boasts / boasting | Promotional copula-avoidance | has, includes, offers |
| navigate (figurative) | "navigating the complexities of…" | handle, deal with, work through |
| foster / fostering | Abstract, bureaucratic | encourage, support, build |
| leverage | Corporate jargon for "use" | use, draw on, tap |
| harness | Same | use, tap into |
| robust | Vague praise | strong, solid, reliable |

### 1b. Inflated "puffery" adjectives

Unearned superlatives applied without evidence. **Fix: delete, or replace with
a concrete specific.**

vibrant, bustling, vital, crucial, essential, paramount, integral, profound,
nuanced, multifaceted, comprehensive, holistic, seamless, exemplary,
ever-evolving, cutting-edge, transformative, groundbreaking, innovative,
game-changing, revolutionary, dynamic, prestigious, renowned, celebrated,
esteemed, bespoke, nascent, invaluable, relentless, unwavering, stark,
noteworthy, meticulous(ly), enduring, distinctive, elegant, refined, keen.

### 1c. Inflated verbs (the thesaurus-upgrade family)

Plain verb dressed up in its formal option. Replacement in parentheses.

delve (explore), leverage (use), utilize/utilise (use), harness (use), elevate
(raise/improve), empower (help/let), unlock/unleash (open up), amplify (boost),
streamline (simplify), revolutionize (change), enhance (improve), facilitate
(help/ease), cultivate (build/grow), foster (encourage), illuminate (show),
underscore (stress), resonate (connect), embark (start), navigate (handle),
unravel (untangle), elucidate (explain), encompass (include/cover), discern
(tell/spot), adhere (follow), optimize (improve), conceptualize (plan),
supercharge (boost), garner (gain/get), bolster (strengthen/back), exemplify
(show), align (match/fit).

### 1d. Abstract / vague nouns

realm, landscape, tapestry, testament, synergy, interplay, array (diverse
array), beacon, intersection, underpinnings, implications, perspectives,
complexity, insights (valuable insights), efficiency, integration,
optimization, metamorphosis, endeavor, heritage, craftsmanship, focal point.

### 1e. Stiff transitions (sprinkled mechanically)

Replacement in parentheses; many are best just cut.

Additionally (also/and), Furthermore (also/plus), Moreover (also/on top of
that), Consequently (so/as a result), Thus (so), Hence (so), Subsequently
(then/later), Nonetheless/Nevertheless (still/even so), Notably (in
particular), Importantly (cut), Alternatively (or), Indeed (cut),
Undoubtedly/Certainly (cut — unearned confidence), Arguably (often cut),
Firstly/Secondly (first/second, or cut).

## 2. Phrase & collocation tells

### 2a. Significance / legacy inflation

Formulaic ways to assert importance without showing it. **Fix: cut the frame,
state the fact.**

- "stands as / serves as a testament to…"
- "is a (powerful) reminder of…" / "as a powerful reminder"
- "plays a pivotal / crucial / vital / key / significant role in…"
- "underscores / highlights the importance (or significance) of…"
- "leaves / left an indelible mark"
- "marks a turning point" / "represents / marks a (paradigm) shift"
- "reflects (a) broader…" / "contributing to the broader…"
- "setting the stage for…" / "paving the way for…"
- "symbolizing its ongoing / enduring / lasting…"

### 2b. "Analysis-flavored" filler

- "providing / offering valuable insights into…"
- "into the complex interplay (of/between)…"
- "delivering actionable insights" / "data-driven decisions"
- "the multifaceted / intricate nature of…"
- "navigating the complexities of…" / "shed light on…"

### 2c. "Today's world" scene-setting openers

Generic throat-clearing. **Fix: delete entirely; start with the actual point.**

- "In today's fast-paced world…" / "In today's digital age…"
- "In today's ever-evolving / rapidly evolving world…"
- "In the age of…" / "In a world where…"
- "In the ever-evolving (landscape of)…" / "In the realm of…"
- "When it comes to…" / "At its core…" / "At the end of the day…"

### 2d. Promotional / travel-brochure puffery

- "nestled in the heart of…" / "nestled within the breathtaking…"
- "rich cultural heritage" / "rich history"
- "a diverse tapestry / diverse array of…"
- "breathtaking" / "stunning natural beauty" / "must-visit"
- "a vibrant / dynamic hub" / "treasure trove of…"
- "unwavering commitment to…" / "commitment to excellence"
- "seamless integration" / "robust solution" / "game-changer"

### 2e. Conclusion / summary templates

- "In conclusion / In summary / In essence / Overall…" (reflexive, even in
  short passages)
- "Despite [its strengths / these challenges], [subject] continues to…"
- "It is clear that…" / "Ultimately…"

### 2f. Signpost hedges

Meta-commentary; a human just says the thing. **Fix: delete the opener, keep
the content.**

- "It's worth noting / important to note that…"
- "It's important to remember / consider that…"
- "One might argue that…" / "It is worth mentioning…"
- "Based on the information provided…" / "As previously mentioned…"
- "Needless to say…"

## 3. Structural & rhetorical tells

These are patterns, not words — often the strongest signals, and the ones a
word list misses.

### Negative parallelism / antithesis
"It's not X, it's Y." / "Not just X, but Y." / "This isn't about X — it's
about Y." A signature rhythmic tic.
- ❌ "It's not just a tool — it's a complete reimagining of the workflow."
- ✅ "It reimagines the workflow." (pick the positive claim; state it once)

### Rule of three / adjective stacking
Triplet lists everywhere; three adjectives where one would do.
- ❌ "a powerful, flexible, and intuitive platform"
- ✅ "a flexible platform" (use the one precise word)

### False range ("from X to Y")
Implies a spectrum that isn't real.
- ❌ "From intimate gatherings to global movements, it brings people together."
- ✅ "It brings people together." (or name the actual scope)

### Em-dash overuse
Dashes deployed for "punchy emphasis" where a comma or period would do.
- ❌ "The result was clear — and it changed everything — for the team."
- ✅ "The result was clear, and it changed everything for the team." (restore
  normal punctuation; vary it; an occasional em-dash is fine)

### Copula avoidance
Instead of "X is Y," AI writes "X serves as / stands as / boasts / represents Y."
- ❌ "The library serves as a hub for the community."
- ✅ "The library is a community hub."

### Compulsive summary
"Overall," / "In conclusion," after only a paragraph or two. → Cut it.

### Formatting tics
Reflexive **bolding** of key terms, emoji as bullet points, headers for every
two sentences, lists where prose would read better. Convert back to flowing
prose unless the format genuinely helps.

### Hollow both-sides conclusion
"The key is balance." / "Ultimately, it depends." / weighing two sides then
refusing to land. → Take a position, or cut the paragraph.

## 4. What NOT to touch (avoid over-editing)

The biggest failure mode of this skill is over-correction. Guard against it:

- **Legitimate uses stay.** A geographer writing about a literal *landscape*, a
  weaver writing about a literal *tapestry*, code that *navigates* a tree — all
  fine. Judge in context.
- **Preserve the author's voice and meaning.** Don't flatten an intentionally
  formal, ornate, or playful style into generic plainness. Match the register
  the writer chose.
- **Don't introduce errors or change claims.** Removing inflation must not
  alter facts, citations, names, or technical meaning.
- **One strong word is fine.** A single "crucial" in a page is not slop. Only
  act on density and reflexive use.
- **Don't swap one tell for another.** Replacing every "delve" with "explore"
  across a document just creates a new tell. Vary, or delete.
- **Domain terms of art are not tells.** "Robust" in statistics, "optimize" in
  ML, "leverage" in finance — keep when technically precise.

When in doubt, prefer the lighter edit and flag it rather than rewrite
aggressively.
